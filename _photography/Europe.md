I started my journey in Paris, where I spent four full days soaking in the city's atmosphere. From there, I took a day trip to the breathtaking Mont-Saint-Michel. Then, I boarded the TGV to Avignon, a charming town in the South of France.

After that, I headed to Barcelona for two days, where I visited most of Gaudí’s iconic architectural masterpieces. Next I flew to Lisbon to explore the historic downtown and the scenic Cascais region. I then took a flight back to Madrid and spent the following three days there - you know, I am always a fan of Real Madrid. 

From Madrid, I took the Renfe train to Andalusia, where I had my most unforgettable experience—visiting the magnificent Alhambra and the passionate Flamenco museum. My journey continued to Rome, where I spent another three days. Finally, I flew to Munich and enjoyed three more days there.
